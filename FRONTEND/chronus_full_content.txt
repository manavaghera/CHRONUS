CHRONUS SLIDE 5: Introduction — What is Chronus?
======================================================================
Layout: DEFAULT

--- TEXT BLOCKS ---
[1] Introduction — What is Chronus?

[2] Chronus is an agentic AI system that simulates the complete cognitive identity of a human — their knowledge, mindset, beliefs, communication style, voice, and personality — by training directly on their personal data, writings, and memories.

[3] Brain-to-AI Training

[4] Personal writings, memories, and beliefs encoded into neural vector representations — mirroring how the brain stores knowledge.

[5] Famous Personality Models

[6] Pre-trained on 8 historical figures using real writings, documented philosophy, and known communication styles.

[7] Voice Identity Cloning

[8] Replicates a person's actual voice using AI synthesis — responses delivered in their real tone, indistinguishable from the real human.

[9] Agentic Reasoning

[10] Goes beyond retrieval — reasons across memories, draws connections between topics, and answers questions the person never explicitly wrote about.

[11] Family Contribution

[12] Multiple contributors — family, friends, colleagues — answer guided interview questions to build a richer, multi-perspective model.

[13] Model Persistence

[14] Every trained model is saved with full history. Resume any conversation with any relative or personality at any time.

CHRONUS SLIDE 8: Problem Statement — The Gap We Address
======================================================================
Layout: DEFAULT

--- TEXT BLOCKS ---
[1] Problem Statement — The Gap We Address

[2] "The human brain accumulates decades of knowledge, experience, and personality — yet all of it vanishes permanently at death, even when vast written, audio, and digital records exist. No system yet captures and re-animates a complete human identity."

[3] Irreversible Knowledge Loss

[4] Billions of human lives end with no AI-accessible record of their intellectual identity, wisdom, or worldview.

[5] No Voice Preservation System

[6] Existing AI chatbots respond in robotic text — they cannot replicate a person's real voice, breaking the illusion of genuine conversation.

[7] Fragmented Digital Archives

[8] Personal diaries, emails, texts, and letters exist in isolated formats. No unified system intelligently queries across all of them.

[9] Grief Without Closure

[10] Bereaved families have no way to hear, interact with, or ask questions of a deceased loved one — a deeply human need technology hasn't solved.

[11] Historical Knowledge Locked

[12] The wisdom of Einstein, Gandhi, or Lincoln is trapped in static texts with no interactive, conversational, voice-enabled interface.

[13] AI Hallucination Risk

[14] Current LLMs role-playing historical figures hallucinate facts. Chronus grounds every response in real, verified writings — not fabricated content.

======================================================================

--- TEXT BLOCKS ---
[1] Implementation — Core Algorithms & Techniques

[2] NLP

[3] Sentence Embedding (Sentence-BERT)

[4] 384-dim vector encoding capturing semantic meaning. Foundation of all memory retrieval in Chronus.

[5] IR

[6] Cosine Similarity Search (ChromaDB)

[7] Top-k semantically similar passages retrieved per query. Threshold 1.45 eliminates irrelevant matches.

[8] Voice

[9] Voice Cloning (Neural TTS)

[10] Speaker embedding from 30–60s audio. XTTS-v2 synthesises replies in the person's real voice.

[11] NLU

[12] Theme Detection (Rule-Based NLU)

[13] Question classified into 7 themes: love, failure, work, fear, purpose, society, self. Selects persona explanation.

[14] NLG

[15] Mix Response Method

[16] Quote → persona explanation → closing signature. All three steps delivered in cloned voice output.

[17] Data

[18] Interview Augmentation

[19] Q&A answers sentence-segmented and embedded with file data. Enriches sparse training from minimal input.

CHRONUS SLIDE 32: Security, Ethics & Privacy — Responsible AI Design
======================================================================
Layout: DEFAULT

--- TEXT BLOCKS ---
[1] Security, Ethics & Privacy — Responsible AI Design

[2] 🔒

[3] Fully Local Architecture

[4] All data — writings, embeddings, voice samples — stored locally. Zero external AI API calls. Your memories never leave your device.

[5] ✅

[6] Consent-First Model

[7] Chronus requires explicit family consent before training on any individual. Models are owner-locked and not transferable without permission.

[8] 🛡️

[9] Anti-Impersonation Guard

[10] Voice cloning is disabled by default. Requires explicit owner authorisation per session. Cannot be used to impersonate someone without access credentials.

[11] 🧠

[12] No Hallucination Policy

[13] Every response is grounded in real retrieved text. If no relevant memory exists, Chronus acknowledges the gap rather than fabricating content.

[14] 💙

[15] Grief Sensitivity Protocol

[16] Recognises emotionally sensitive queries (grief, loss, death). Activates compassion-mode responses — no clinical detachment when discussing deeply personal topics.

[17] 🗑️

[18] Data Deletion Rights

[19] Every trained model and voice sample can be permanently deleted at any time — full right to be forgotten, implemented at the system level, not just in policy.
